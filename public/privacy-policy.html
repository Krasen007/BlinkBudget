Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026  ·  Effective Date: July 25, 2026

BlinkBudget is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your financial data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our expense tracking application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Financial Transaction Data

  • Transaction Details: Amount, date, description, category, and account information
  • Account Information: Account names, types, and balances
  • Categorization Data: Custom categories and spending patterns
  • Timestamps: Creation and modification dates for records

1.2 User Authentication Data

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and login when you sign in
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure session identifiers managed by Firebase Auth when authentication is enabled
  • User ID: Unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication or local user context

1.3 Application Usage Data

  • Settings and Preferences: User-configured app settings and display preferences
  • Internal Usage Data: Anonymous app usage metrics collected for feature improvement when enabled
  • Error Logs: Technical data for debugging and performance improvement

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Service Provision

  • Expense Tracking: Store and organize your financial transactions
  • Data Synchronization: Sync data across devices when a cloud account is active
  • Account Management: Manage multiple financial accounts and categories
  • Reporting: Generate financial reports and insights

2.2 Security and Performance

  • Authentication: Verify your identity and secure access to your data when you sign in
  • Data Protection: Implement security measures to protect your information
  • Service Improvement: Analyze anonymous usage and error data to improve the app

3. Data Storage and Architecture

3.1 Hybrid Storage Model

BlinkBudget uses a hybrid local-cloud architecture designed for privacy and performance:

Local Storage (Primary):

  • All financial data is primarily stored in your browser's localStorage
  • Data remains on your device and is accessible offline
  • No data is transmitted to cloud servers unless you opt in to cloud sync and sign in

Security Limitations of localStorage:

  • localStorage is not encrypted by the app and is stored in plain text on your device
  • Data is accessible to any script running on the same origin
  • Anyone with physical access to your device may access stored data

Cloud Storage (Optional):

  • Cloud synchronization is available only when you create an account and log in
  • Cloud storage uses Firebase Firestore for backup and cross-device sync when configured
  • You can use the app entirely offline without creating an account

3.2 Data Encryption and Security

  • Local Data: Stored in browser localStorage and managed by the browser
  • Cloud Data: Encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and protected by Firebase Firestore security
  • Authentication: Managed by Firebase Authentication when enabled
  • Access Control: User data is isolated by user ID when cloud sync is active

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 No Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial transaction data with third parties for advertising or marketing by default
  • Your transaction data is not used for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Financial transaction data is not transmitted to unrelated analytics companies when running in local-only mode; optional telemetry and error reporting may be transmitted if you enable those features

4.2 Service Providers

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication and optional cloud storage when configured; acts as a cloud processor when cloud sync is enabled
  • Netlify: Used for application hosting in many deployments and may process deployment analytics when enabled
  • These providers are used only to support the app's functionality and sync features

4.3 Legal Requirements

  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights
  • Any such disclosure is limited to what is legally required

5. Data Retention

5.1 Local Data

  • Data stored in localStorage remains until you clear it
  • You can delete all data through the app's data management features
  • Clearing browser data will remove locally stored information

5.2 Cloud Data

  • Cloud data is retained while your account is active
  • You can delete your account and all associated cloud data through the app
  • Backup copies may persist briefly as part of provider retention policies

Account Deletion Process

When you delete your account, the app attempts to:

  • Generate a final data export for your records when possible
  • Permanently delete transactions, accounts, goals, investments, budgets, and settings
  • Remove authentication data and sign you out
  • Clean up related local and cloud state

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Access and Control

  • View: You can view your stored data through the app interface
  • Export: You can export your data at any time using built-in export features
  • Delete: You can delete individual records, all data, or your account
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and associated data through the app

6.2 Local-Only Usage

  • You can use BlinkBudget offline without creating an account
  • No financial transaction data is transmitted to external servers when running locally; optional telemetry and error logs may still be transmitted if you enable those features. When cloud features are used, providers such as Firebase and Netlify act as cloud processors.
  • All features are available except cloud sync and account-based features

6.3 Opt-Out Choices

  • Analytics: You can disable optional usage data collection in privacy settings
  • Cloud Sync: You can disable cloud synchronization while keeping local data
  • Marketing: The app does not send marketing communications by default

7. Security Measures

7.1 Technical Security

  • Encryption: Cloud data is encrypted in transit
  • Authentication: Secure sign-in is available when Firebase Authentication is enabled
  • Input Validation: User inputs are validated and sanitized where applicable

7.2 Access Controls

  • User Isolation: Each signed-in user can only access their own data
  • Session Management: Authentication session state is managed by Firebase when enabled
  • Audit Logging: The app may keep internal logs for diagnostic purposes

7.3 Data Integrity

  • Backup: Optional cloud backup is available when signed in
  • Conflict Resolution: Sync logic aims to resolve simultaneous edits safely
  • Data Validation: The app performs checks to help keep stored data consistent

8. Children's Privacy

BlinkBudget is not intended for children below the applicable age of digital consent in their jurisdiction. In the United States this is generally under 13 years old under COPPA. Under GDPR Article 8 the default age of digital consent is 16, although EU Member States may lawfully lower that age to 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age in their jurisdiction. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

If you use cloud synchronization, your data may be stored and processed in data centers located outside your country. Firebase implements safeguards for international data transfers in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be posted in the repository and, when applicable, communicated through app release notes or GitHub updates.

Material Changes

A change is material if it affects:

  • How we collect or use personal data
  • The purposes for which we use personal data
  • Your rights regarding personal data
  • Who we share personal data with

Notification Process

  • Updates will be posted in the repository and project documentation
  • Major changes may be noted in release notes or update notifications
  • Updated policy will be available at the published privacy policy URL

11. Contact Information

Public Channels (General Inquiries)

  • GitHub Issues: Create an issue in our repository
  • Documentation: Available in the project's published documentation and on the hosted site at the published documentation URLs

Important: Do not submit sensitive personal information through public GitHub issues.

12. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Portability: Transfer your data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to certain data processing
  • Right to Restrict: Limit processing of your data
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority if you believe your rights under GDPR have been violated

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under CCPA:

  • Right to Know: What personal information is collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of sale of personal information (the app does not sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights
Note: This Privacy Policy is designed to be consistent with major privacy frameworks. BlinkBudget prioritizes user privacy by keeping data primarily on your device and only using cloud sync when explicitly enabled.
