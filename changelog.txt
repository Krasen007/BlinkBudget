# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- **Tags and labels load offline at startup** - On a cold start the tag list
  stayed empty until Firebase auth resolved (which can stall entirely without
  network), so a new transaction could not be tagged. Local data is now read
  synchronously via the last signed-in user id, and an open form refreshes its
  tag list as soon as categories become available.
- **Budgets scale with the selected report period** - Selecting a quarter or a
  year in Reports compared the whole period's expenses against a single
  month's budget, so everything looked wildly over budget. Monthly budgets are
  now scaled to the selected period (a quarter gets x3, a year x12), the
  category cards use the scaled limit, and the summary card notes the scaling.

## [1.49] - 2026-09-25

A release focused on making the Insights experience more honest and more
useful, plus a batch of correctness fixes around refunds, accounts, and
error reporting.

### Added

- **Savings Rate on the Trend chart** - A new amber dashed line tracks your savings
  rate on its own percentage axis, so you can see whether the money you keep
  actually grows month over month.
- **Unusual spending flags in chart tooltips** - Hovering a month or a day now
  tells you how many outlier expenses it contained, instead of a percentage that
  made no sense for a time series.
- **Recurring & Subscriptions card** - BlinkBudget spots fixed-price, regular
  spending (rent, subscriptions, memberships) and lists each one with its
  cadence, amount, and the next expected date.
- **Key Takeaways strip** - The three most relevant insights for the month you
  are browsing, ranked by severity, so you do not have to read every card.
- **Today tab arrows in Reports** - The Today period now has the same
  left/right navigation as This Month, Last Month, This Quarter and This Year,
  letting you step back to Yesterday and beyond.
- **Both percentages in category tooltips** - When you hide categories in a
  chart, the tooltip shows the original share and the recalculated share, so the
  remaining numbers still add up to something meaningful.
- **Data Integrity Report in Settings** - A quick, readable summary of what the
  app found when it last checked your stored data.
- **Honest export feedback** - If a backup was only partially assembled, the
  dialog now says so and names what is missing, instead of claiming success.
- **Tags on the Add Transaction form** - Pick a tag while logging, without
  having to open an existing transaction first.

### Fixed

- **Refunds now appear in Reports** - They were saved correctly but then hidden
  from the report charts, so refund-heavy months looked emptier than they were.
- **Editing an account no longer wipes it** - Saving an account used to reset its
  balance and creation date. Both are now preserved, with a regression test.
- **Tags render offline** - Transaction labels disappeared without an internet
  connection; they now come from local data.
- **Charts fail visibly** - If a chart cannot be built, you get a readable
  fallback message instead of a blank card.
- **Account list failures are recoverable** - A rendering error now shows an
  inline alert with a Retry button rather than an empty list, and Retry never
  re-saves your data behind your back.
- **Error dialogs always reach you** - If the dialog bundle fails to load, a
  fallback still shows the message instead of failing silently.
- **Restore failures are logged** - A failed restore now leaves a real trace in
  the console alongside the alert you already saw.
- **Chart memory leak closed** - Charts are now destroyed when you switch
  financial-planning sections, so long sessions no longer accumulate dead
  charts.

### Changed

- **Trend chart is easier to read** - Income (green) and expenses (red) are
  drawn as one centered column per period instead of two side-by-side bars.
- **Removed duplicate Insights surfaces** - The repeated KPI summary bar and the
  overlapping Daily Expenses section are gone, leaving one clear view.
- **Clearer naming** - The Reports section is now "Spending Insights", so the app
  no longer has two different screens called "Financial Insights".
- **Fully English charts** - All chart titles and labels are English, with
  consistent currency formatting shared across the app.
- **Top Movers show real movement** - Signed month-over-month deltas (green for
  spending less, red for spending more) with a tooltip explaining the change.
- **Cleanup under the hood** - ESLint was updated to a flat config, unused
  subsystems and duplicate helpers were removed, and date normalization was
  consolidated so a restored backup behaves exactly like a fresh load.
