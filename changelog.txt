## [1.46.0] - 2026-02-21

### Added

- **Progressive Unlock System** - Advanced financial planning features now unlock progressively as you log transactions, connecting your 3-click habit to powerful insights:
  - **Smart Budget Suggestions** (30+ transactions) - Auto-suggested category budgets based on your actual spending history
  - **Goal Cash Flow Insights** (30+ transactions) - Connect spending patterns to long-term savings goals
  - **Deep Analytics & Trends** (30+ transactions) - View top expense movers, category trends, and personal inflation analysis
  - **Income & Expense Forecasting** (90+ transactions) - Unlock 6-month balance and cash flow predictions
  - **Portfolio & Net Worth Sync** (30+ transactions) - See how portfolio holdings interact with overall net worth
  - **Financial Health Assessment** (30+ transactions) - Get accurate emergency fund coverage assessment and risk score

- **Automatic Budget Suggestions** - When you have 30+ transactions, BlinkBudget automatically suggests category budgets based on your spending history with Accept, Adjust, or Dismiss options

### Changed

- **Goals Section Optimization** - Moved and optimized the goals section as part of "The One Theory" implementation, connecting the 3-click transaction logging habit to advanced financial planning features
- **Budget Updates** - Enhanced budget management with better integration of automatic suggestions
- **Anomaly Detection** - Improved AnomalyService for better spending pattern analysis

### Fixed

- Animation issues when adding transactions
- Property test failures
- All verification checklist items complete (404/404 tests passing, 47/47 files)

### Maintenance
